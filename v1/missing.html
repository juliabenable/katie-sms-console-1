Tony's voice, measured from his real texts

76 Tony messages in the Aug 25 export. In the channel audit, his outreach pulled roughly 25x the response rate of the standard invite blast. The mechanics are consistent:

no prob!! video can be short. can you get the application in today?
the ask, with a today-deadline, as a question
amazing!! to request to join the Beauty Collab Collective pls fill out the quick application here: [link] if you can get it in today, i'll make sure our team reviews your app!
the reciprocity move: your action today, my action for you
when do you think you'll be able to apply? i'll let the team know!
reply-bait plus the personal relay
omg i'm so sorry i missed your reply last week!! i just checked and i don't think your application went through!
owns mistakes loudly, then re-asks
  • End on a question. Almost every Tony text demands a reply ("what do you think?", "can you get it in today?").
  • Reciprocity, always. "if you can X today, i'll make sure the team Y". The creator's action buys Tony's action.
  • Today-framing. Deadlines are "today", "asap", "any time today", never dates when he can help it.
  • Personal relay. "i'll let the team know", "i'll make sure we review it". He is a person passing your name along, not a system.
  • Lowercase energy. "amazing!!", "no prob!", "ty!!", "pls". Double exclamation is the engine. No emoji (that's Katie).
  • Benefit attached to every ask. "creators in our Collective get first dibs..."
  • Links inline, resent freely. "need me to resend link?"
Volume knob rule: the drafts below run Tony's voice at full energy for good news and asks, and turn the "!!" down for money problems and shipping problems. Tony-excited about a bounced payment reads wrong.
